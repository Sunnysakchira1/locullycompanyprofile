Step 01

AI Visibility Audit

We analyse your brand across all major AI platforms and benchmark it against competitors. This establishes a clear baseline and identifies the specific gaps driving missed recommendations — including the technical blocks most sites do not know they have.

Step 02

Technical & Content Foundation

On-page SEO, schema markup, and structured content specifically formatted to be cited by AI models. We build the pages that AI engines pull from when answering user queries.

Step 03

Authority & Backlink Building

Editorial placements on DR50+ publications, digital PR, and citation building across authoritative directories. The trust signals that AI and Google both require to recommend a source.

Step 04

Monitoring & Compounding

Every 72 hours we re-run your prompt set across live AI search and store the full answer text — not a score, the actual response. Visibility compounds: each asset built continues to earn citations and rankings beyond the campaign period.

Case Evidence — IVF Clinic, Bangkok

A SET-listed IVF clinic, invisible to every AI engine. The cause was four lines in a config file.

A fertility clinic listed on the Stock Exchange of Thailand — 2,000+ patients a year, 60–65% ICSI success rate, three branches, a seven-doctor team. In June 2026 it appeared in zero AI answers on any engine. Not because the clinic was weak — because its robots.txt was blocking GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and CCBot from reading the site at all. A default setting nobody had chosen. We found it, specified the fix, and rebuilt the English content stack on top of it.

Before · the cause
Eight AI crawlers set to Disallow: / — GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, CCBot and more. The models could not read the site.
Before · the effect
“Top IVF clinics in Bangkok” on ChatGPT. Five clinics recommended. The SET-listed one is not among them.
After · two months
0 → 242 AI responses citing the clinic across 81 pages — 141 in Google AI Overviews, plus AI Mode, Perplexity, Copilot, ChatGPT and Gemini.
After · two months
Search Console, June vs August: impressions 15.8K → 153K, clicks 99 → 487, average position 32.7 → 16.3.

August is a 28-day window against June’s 30 — the growth is understated, not flattered. Engagement began 9 June 2026, so June is the pre-engagement baseline and the result represents two months of delivery.
