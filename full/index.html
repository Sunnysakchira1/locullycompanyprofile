About Locully

A Bangkok Agency for GEO, SEO and Performance Marketing

Locully is a digital marketing agency in Bangkok, Thailand. We help businesses get found where their customers search, compare and make decisions: Google Search, Google Maps and AI assistants like ChatGPT.

GEO, or AI search, is our core work. Locully works on how AI assistants find, understand and describe your business. That includes ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

Locully’s approach is pull marketing. Instead of pushing messages at people and hoping they care, we help you get found by people who are already searching for what you sell.

  1. Measure. Locully records where you stand: Google rankings, Google Maps, and what AI assistants say about you on a fixed set of buying questions.
  2. Fix. Locully repairs the site, the content and the business details that search engines and AI assistants read.
  3. Place. Locully earns mentions of your business on trusted third-party sites, the kind Google and AI assistants read.
  4. Report. Every month you get a report tied to enquiries, bookings and cost per acquisition.
